Why Gulf solar first

FlytBase already has the proof. EnBW scaled solar inspections from 150 MW to 1 GW with docks and got 2 to 4 times more inspections for the same budget. A buyer in Abu Dhabi can picture that.

The region is already in motion. The featured case study on the site is Ibn Firnas in Oman, there is a UAE waste authority story, and Aeronex became master distributor for MENA in May 2025. Partners need meetings to sell into.

The timing is public. The accounts below cover over 22 GW of new solar that is being built, has been financed, or is out to bid. Every one of those sites will need an inspection and security plan, and the people writing it can be found today.

How I built it

Picking the segment

I read FlytBase's 23 customer stories and the BDR job post, which names energy, mining and critical infrastructure. Solar had the clearest number (EnBW), and the Gulf had the most news in 2026. So I picked the overlap instead of a broad list.

Finding triggers

I used Claude with web search to pull project news, then opened every source myself and wrote the trigger in my own words. Where a source only reports a frontrunner, the row says so. One row has no fresh trigger and says that too.

What I left out

No names or contact details: a rep would find the person in Sales Navigator and check that the role exists. I also did not check drone rules (GCAA in the UAE, GACA in Saudi) per site; that is the next thing I would research before sending. My outbound agent can run the research step at scale.
